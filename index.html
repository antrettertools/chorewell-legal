Chorewell

Kids earn screen time and pocket money by doing chores. Parents send chores, approve them with photo proof, and set daily screen-time, bedtime and school-time rules. On Android, the kid's phone locks by itself when time is up.

  • No ads, no tracking, no data selling.
  • Proof photos are deleted as soon as a parent reviews them.
  • Parents can delete everything at any time.
