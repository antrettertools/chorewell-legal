Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Chorewell is provided by Samuel Antretter, Steinbrucker-str.11, 83064 Raubling, Germany ("we", "us"). We are the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact for anything privacy-related: antrettertools@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • A parent creates the family account and controls everything. Kids never create an account or enter an email.
  • We collect only what the app needs to work: the parent's email, what you enter about your family, and — on a kid's Android phone with screen-time rules on — how many minutes the phone was used per day, which apps were used and for how long, and when it was locked.
  • Proof photos are deleted as soon as you approve or reject the chore.
  • No ads. No analytics or tracking tools. No selling or sharing of data. We never collect your child's location, contacts, messages, or what they do inside an app, including which websites they visit.
  • You can delete a child's data or your whole account inside the app at any time.

3. What we process, why, and on what legal basis

Parent account: email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our hosting provider), display name, and a record of when you accepted this policy and our terms. Purpose: your account and sign-in. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); the consent record also serves our legal obligation to be able to demonstrate consent (Art. 6(1)(c), Art. 7(1) GDPR).

Family data you enter: your children's first names and birthdays (used to show their age), chores, rewards, token and allowance balances and history, screen-time rules, and extra-time requests including the short reason your child may type. Purpose: providing the app to your family. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and, for data about your children, the consent you give as their parent or legal guardian (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 8 GDPR).

Proof photos: if a chore needs photo proof, your child takes a photo in the app. It is stored privately and shown only to the parent account, and deleted automatically as soon as you approve or reject that chore. Leftover files (for example after a failed upload) are removed the next time a parent opens the app. Please ask your child to photograph the finished chore, not people. Legal basis: as for family data.

Kid phone data: a kid's phone signs in with a random, anonymous ID (no name, email or phone number). It reports the platform and app version, whether screen-time protection is switched on, the total minutes the phone was used each day, and a log of when the phone was locked and unlocked by the rules, including if the phone's clock or time zone was changed or protection was switched off. Purpose: enforcing the screen-time rules you set and showing them to you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and your consent as parent (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 8 GDPR).

App activity: if a parent turns on Android's Usage access permission for Chorewell on a kid's phone, the phone also reports the name of each app used and how long it was used per day, and the parent can see this in the app. A browser appears as one app; we never receive which websites were visited, or anything that happens inside an app. Purpose: showing you how screen time was spent. The same permission lets Chorewell check on the phone which app is open, so it can show the time's-up page; that check isn't uploaded. Legal basis: your consent as parent (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 8 GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by turning that permission off. Setup explains this on the kid's phone, and a notification is always shown there while rules are active.

Server logs: when the app talks to our servers, our hosting provider briefly processes technical data such as IP address and time of access to operate and secure the service. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling tools of any kind.
  • No access to your child's location, contacts, messages or browsing history, or to what happens inside an app.
  • No automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR — apps are blocked or the phone locks only according to the rules you set.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone for their own purposes.

5. Data stored on the device

The app stores your sign-in session, your settings (such as the colour theme) and, on a kid's phone, the screen-time rules and today's usage so the rules keep working offline. This is strictly necessary to provide the app you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG) and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

6. Who processes data for us

Supabase Inc. hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Our project is located in Stockholm, Sweden (eu-north-1). Where Supabase or its sub-processors may access data from outside the EU/EEA, the transfer is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Google distributes the app through Google Play. When you download it, Google processes data under its own privacy policy; we only receive aggregated, anonymous statistics and crash reports from Google Play.

If you email us, your email provider and ours (Google Gmail) process your message so we can answer it.

7. How long we keep data

  • Proof photos: until you approve or reject the chore (then deleted immediately).
  • App activity: 35 days at most, then deleted automatically.
  • Everything else: as long as your family account exists. Removing a child deletes all of that child's data. Deleting your account deletes all of your family's data immediately.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups, if any, are overwritten on the provider's regular backup cycle.
  • Server logs are kept only briefly by our hosting provider for security.

8. Children

Chorewell is meant to be set up and controlled by a parent or legal guardian, who must be at least 18. Children join only with the family code from their parent and do not create accounts. By setting up Chorewell for your children you confirm that you have parental responsibility for them and consent to the processing described here. You can see, correct and delete your children's data in the app at any time.

For families in the United States: we collect information about children only through, and with the consent of, their parent, as described above, and only to provide the app (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

9. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)) — for example by removing a child or deleting your account in the app. Email antrettertools@gmail.com for anything you can't do in the app.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one where you live or the one responsible for us: Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA).

10. Deleting your data

  • Delete a child: Parent app → the child's page → Danger zone → Remove.
  • Delete your whole account and family: Parent app → Settings → Delete account. This happens immediately.
  • Can't open the app any more? Email antrettertools@gmail.com from your account's email address and we'll delete everything within 30 days.

11. Security

All connections are encrypted (TLS). Every family's data is separated by access rules on the database, so one family can never see another's. Photos are stored privately and are only viewable through short-lived links.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above. If the change affects what we do with your data, the app asks you to accept the new version.
