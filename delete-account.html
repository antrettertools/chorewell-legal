Delete your Chorewell account

Deleting your account permanently removes your parent account and all of your family's data: children's profiles, chores, proof photos, tokens, allowance, screen-time rules and history. Your kids' phones are unlinked and their screen-time rules stop.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Chorewell on the parent phone.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete account, tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Without the app

Email antrettertools@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject “Delete my Chorewell account”. We delete all data within 30 days and confirm by email.

To delete only one child's data: Parent app → the child's page → Danger zone → Remove.
